General Information

What are the age requirements to start driving lessons? expand_more
Students must be at least 15 years and 6 months old to begin classroom instruction, and 16 years old to begin behind-the-wheel training. A valid learner's permit is required before scheduling any in-car lessons.
What documents do I need to bring to my first lesson? expand_more
For your first behind-the-wheel lesson, you must bring your valid physical learner's permit. Digital copies are not accepted by state law. You should also wear closed-toe shoes and bring any corrective lenses if required by your permit.

Lessons & Scheduling

How long is each driving lesson? expand_more
Our standard behind-the-wheel lessons are 90 minutes long. We have found this duration optimal for maintaining focus while allowing enough time to practice multiple maneuvers and routes.
Do you offer pickup and drop-off services? expand_more
Yes, we offer complimentary pickup and drop-off from home, school, or work within a 10-mile radius of our main training center. Extended range pickup is available for an additional fee.

Pricing & Payments

What payment methods do you accept? expand_more
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, and secure online payments through our student portal. We do not accept personal checks or cash for online bookings.
